About the OS

MOPS Operating System

The operational home base for Marketing Operations at Thumbtack.

Vision

"A single home for how MOPS thinks, plans, and operates — where the work happens, not just where it's documented."

Mission

"To bring MOPS's processes, tools, intelligence, and knowledge into one place — so the team can move faster, work smarter, and make better decisions."

The Problem

As MOPS grows in scope and complexity, the tools and context needed to operate well are spread across multiple platforms. Briefs live in Google Docs, processes live in Drive, onboarding lives in static files. There's no single place that ties it all together.

The Opportunity

By centralizing not just documentation but actual operational tools — brief analysis, QA workflows, capacity monitoring, onboarding — MOPS can run a more consistent, scalable, and intelligent operation from one place.

The Solution

MOPS OS is the operational home base for Marketing Operations. It hosts the tools MOPS uses to plan and assess campaigns, the processes that guide execution, the knowledge that supports the team, and the intelligence that drives better decisions. It's not a replacement for deployment platforms like Iterable or Pardot — it's the front door to all of them, and everything that happens before, around, and after them.

Beyond the team itself, MOPS OS is also built for the stakeholders and leaders who work with us. It's a window into how MOPS operates — the scope of what we support, the standards we hold, and the capabilities we bring to the table — so that those who partner with MOPS can engage with us more effectively, and those who lead us can see the full picture.

How to Submit & What to Expect
1
Submission

Once you've identified the right form, submit your request. After submitting, you'll receive an Asana notification and an email confirming your submission — including a link to the generated campaign brief and Asana task.

MOPS typically needs at least 3–4 weeks notice before the desired launch date. If launching sooner, please share context on the urgency so we can assess feasibility.
2
Campaign Brief Generation

After submission, a campaign brief is automatically generated and linked in your email notification. Some fields auto-populate from the form — you'll need to complete the remaining details. Once done, mark the "Campaign Brief Completed" subtask as complete to notify all teams they can officially begin work.

While brief completion is required before MOPS officially kicks off, if there's enough information and deliverables are already available, MOPS may begin work on parts of the campaign. Your assigned MOPS owner will reach out to clarify details and align on how to proceed.
Please be mindful when selecting the support needed in the form. If you submit without selecting MOPS or Brand support, no campaign brief will be generated and your request will not be routed to the appropriate team. Make sure to select one or both.
3
Review Schedule

Requests can be submitted any time, any day. A campaign will be assigned to a MOPS owner within or after 24 hours of receiving the request. The assigned MOPS owner will then review, assess, and acknowledge the request within 24–48 hours after assignment.

Watch out for updates on #marketing-ops-xfn ↗

4
Project Initiation

Once the campaign brief is completed and all deliverables are provided, the involved teams will begin working on your campaign or project.

Campaign Brief Completion
Requester Required to proceed

MOPS will not officially begin work until the campaign brief is fully completed by the requester. This is a hard requirement — the brief is the foundation for everything that follows.

5
MOPS Campaign Review & Assessment
MOPS

Even before the brief is fully completed, the assigned MOPS owner may do a quick preliminary review to assess timelines and flag gaps. Early assessment helps with capacity forecasting and gives stakeholders realistic timelines upfront.

Build & Development Campaign assets are built and prepared for review
6
Advance Assessment & Stakeholder Thread
MOPS

Regardless of launch date, the MOPS owner reviews the campaign brief and opens a Slack thread in #marketing-ops-xfn ↗ to acknowledge the request. This surfaces pending assets, flags missing information, and sets realistic timelines.

7
Campaign Asset Build
MOPS

The MOPS owner builds all campaign assets — emails, push notifications, in-app messages, and landing pages — based on the approved brief. All assets are built for both design and dynamic functionality.

Stage 1 — MOPS Owner Self-Review
MOPS Owner

Before anything goes to stakeholders, the MOPS owner conducts a thorough self-review of all campaign assets — verifying design, copy, links, dynamic content, and full journey functionality.

9
Stage 2 — Stakeholder Proofing
Requester MOPS

Proofs are sent to stakeholders via email with a shared feedback document and clear deadline. Once all feedback is applied, the MOPS owner closes the thread. Purpose: Brand creative sign-off and Campaign Owner content approval.

10
Stage 3 — MOPS Peer Review & QA
MOPS Team

A rotating quarterly reviewer checks all assets and automation using the QA checklist. A final review by a Lane POC or Manager is optional for complex or high-priority campaigns.

Launch Final checks and campaign goes live
11
Pre-Launch Final Checklist
MOPS

Before launch, the MOPS owner confirms QA is complete, stakeholder approval is received, send time and timezone are correct, and the campaign is set to the right audience.

12
Campaign Launch
MOPS

Campaign goes live at the confirmed send time. The MOPS owner monitors the initial send to confirm delivery is progressing as expected.

Post-Launch Ensuring the campaign completed successfully
13
24-Hour Post-Launch Check
MOPS Mandatory

Within 24 hours of launch, the MOPS owner confirms the campaign completed as expected — checking delivery rates, send completion, and any journey anomalies. Issues should be flagged immediately to the Lane POC and escalated to the Manager if needed.

Best Practices
💡 Start early, even before the brief is done — a quick advance review surfaces blockers early.
💬 Always open a Slack thread to acknowledge the request — it creates a communication trail and keeps everyone aligned.
✉️ Use the proofing email to set expectations — include the feedback deadline and the type of input you need.
🔍 Never skip the 24-hour post-launch check — a campaign launching is not the same as a campaign completing.

Welcome to the team
MOPS Associate New Hire Playbook

Welcome to Marketing Operations at Thumbtack! This playbook is your guide for the first few weeks on the job — and a reference you'll keep coming back to long after onboarding ends. It covers how MOPS works, who you'll work with, the tools you'll use, and what success looks like in your role.

What Marketing Operations Does
Our role at Thumbtack

MOPS is responsible for the end-to-end execution of marketing campaigns — from the moment a request comes in to the moment a campaign goes live. We work closely with Lifecycle Marketing, Product Marketing, Brand, MarTech, Engineering, and Data Science to deliver campaigns accurately, on time, and aligned with business goals.

Within MOPS, the team operates across three lanes — Enablement, Customer, and PRO — each with a dedicated POC. We also continuously improve our processes and workflows to keep up with the pace of the business.

Think of MOPS as the bridge between marketing strategy and technical execution — we make sure ideas become campaigns, and campaigns get delivered right.

Core Responsibilities
Campaign Execution
  • Journey builds and configuration
  • Audience segmentation and validation
  • Asset development and proofing
  • Experiment setup via A/B test or Statsig
Quality and Standards
  • QA checks and peer reviews
  • Post-launch performance monitoring
  • Process documentation and improvement
  • Cross-functional stakeholder coordination
Enablement
  • New capability rollout and readiness
  • Process design and documentation
  • Knowledge transfers across lanes
  • AI and process improvement initiatives
Your role as a new Associate
As an incoming MOPS Associate, your work will sit in the Customer or PRO lane — focused on campaign execution and deployment. Enablement is a separate lane led by a dedicated POC. You'll collaborate with it, but your day-to-day is in deployment.
The Marketing Operations Team
Collaborative, supportive, and always happy to help

MOPS operates through three connected lanes — Enablement, Customer, and PRO. Each lane has a POC responsible for its operational health. Enablement focuses on introducing and operationalizing new capabilities, while Customer and PRO apply those capabilities within their respective campaign and stakeholder contexts.

As a new MOPS Associate, you'll be assigned to a lane. Everyone is approachable and happy to help — so don't hesitate to ask questions, no matter how basic they seem. Asking early saves everyone time.

Who We Usually Work With
Your cross-functional partners across Thumbtack

MOPS works closely with a wide range of cross-functional teams to plan, build, and launch campaigns. These partners provide campaign strategy, content, creative assets, audience targeting, and performance insights — MOPS handles execution and delivery. Strong collaboration and clear communication with these teams is essential to keeping campaigns on track.

What comes next
Here's what your first 30 days look like.

Your onboarding is structured across 4 weeks — moving from learning to shadowing to doing. Each week has a clear goal. Your manager will walk you through the schedule on Day 1, but here's the big picture so you know what to expect.

4-Week Overview
Week 1
Foundations

Learn how MOPS works, how campaigns flow end-to-end, what tools exist, and who you'll work with. Heavy on sessions, recordings, and self-reads.

Week 2
Guided Practice

Start thinking like MOPS. Apply what you've learned through guided scenarios — intake, audience setup, experiment configuration, QA, and end-to-end deployment.

Week 3
Shadow & Assist

Move from "I understand" to "I can do with support." Shadow real campaigns, assist on QA and audience validation, and join stakeholder conversations.

Week 4
Ramp Up

Begin executing basic campaign requests with guidance. Onboarding slows down — but learning doesn't stop. You'll continue shadowing complex work alongside independent execution.

MOPS Campaign Timeline & Best Practices

A campaign timeline makes sure that our team understands what needs to be done, when, and (crucially) why. Having this accountability ensures that everything is delivered potentially with time to spare, and we're prepared for any speed bumps we might encounter along the way.

Authors Seann Demafeliz · Jane Yu · Jae Alcantara
Created Aug 9, 2021
Last Updated Mar 20, 2023
01
MOPS Request Form Submission
First step in getting a project on the team's radar
1
Submit the Asana Intake Form
Requester
›

All new request form submissions are automatically added to the MOPS Asana board. This is the first step to getting a campaign on MOPS's radar.

For urgent campaigns that also need Creative/Brand support, submit the Asana form and select "Yes" under "Are you submitting at least 3 weeks before your desired launch date?" to escalate to the Urgent queue.
02
Brief Submission & Creative Hand-off
Campaign details locked before build begins
2
MOPS Brief Submission
Requester
›

The brief contains all campaign-specific information: audience, deliverables, and launch details. MOPS will not officially begin work until the brief is complete.

3
Creative Hand-off
Brand / Creative
›

The Creative team passes Figma links and image files to MOPS. At the time of delivery, all links, copy, and images must be final and approved by Legal.

MOPS must verify all creative assets are delivered by EOD Tuesday PST (Wednesday PH time) of the week prior to the scheduled launch. If assets are missing, MOPS must inform stakeholders that the campaign may need to be postponed.
